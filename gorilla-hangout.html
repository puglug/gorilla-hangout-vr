🦍 Gorilla Hangout

How do you want to play?

Choose VR on your Quest to request immersive mode, or choose Desktop to use keyboard and mouse.

Gorilla Hangout

VR: swing your arms and push off the ground, walls and tree trunks. Flick the right stick to turn. Hop the floating platforms to reach the top of the tower.

Desktop: mouse to look, WASD to walk, Space to jump, click to shove yourself forward.

Single-player build: online multiplayer only works in the claude.ai version.

Same mode means same lobby, so you only see players in your mode. Tag: touch someone's head to pass IT. Infection: touch to infect, and the round ends when everyone is infected. In the world, hit a button on the stump board (or press 1-4 on desktop). Night Shift teleports you into the pizzeria: the animatronics only move when you are not looking at them. Emotes: B beats your chest and V changes your face (VR: right A changes your face, right B or hugging both hands to your chest beats).
